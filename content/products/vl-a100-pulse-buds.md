# Pulse Buds (VL-A100)

## Overview

Pulse Buds are our everyday true wireless earbuds. They are light, sweat resistant and last a full working day on one charge. They do not have active noise cancellation; for that, see Pulse Buds Pro (VL-A300).

## Features

- Up to 8 hours of playback per charge, and 32 hours in total with the charging case
- Quick charge: 10 minutes in the case gives 1.5 hours of playback
- IPX4 sweat and splash resistance
- Touch controls for play, pause, skip, volume and voice assistant
- Bluetooth 5.3 with SBC and AAC codecs
- Charges over USB-C

# SafeView Indoor Camera (VL-C420)

## Overview

The SafeView Indoor Camera watches a whole room. It pans and tilts from the Voltline Home app, sees in the dark, and has a privacy mode that turns the lens to face the wall.

## Features

- 2K video (2560 by 1440 pixels)
- Pan 340 degrees and tilt 90 degrees from the app
- Infrared night vision up to 10 meters (33 feet)
- Two-way audio
- Motion, sound and person detection with phone alerts
- Privacy mode turns the lens away and stops recording
- Local recording to a microSD card up to 256 GB (card not included)
- Optional cloud recording with a Voltline Secure plan

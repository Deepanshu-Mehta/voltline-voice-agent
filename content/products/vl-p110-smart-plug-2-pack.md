# Smart Plug 2-Pack (VL-P110)

## Overview

Two smart plugs that switch lamps and appliances from your phone, on a schedule or by voice. They measure how much energy each device uses. They support Matter, so they work with Apple Home, Amazon Alexa and Google Home.

## Features

- Two plugs in one pack
- Rated 15 amps, up to 1,800 watts per plug
- Energy monitoring in the Voltline Home app
- Schedules, timers and an away mode that switches lights at random
- Matter support: Apple Home, Amazon Alexa, Google Home
- Indoor use only

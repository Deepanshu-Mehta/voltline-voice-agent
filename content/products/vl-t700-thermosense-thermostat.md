# ThermoSense Thermostat (VL-T700)

## Overview

ThermoSense learns when you are home and adjusts the temperature to save energy. It works with most 24-volt heating and cooling systems. It needs a C-wire (common wire) for power; a C-wire adapter is not included.

## Features

- 3.5-inch color touchscreen
- Occupancy sensor that switches to eco temperatures when nobody is home
- Monthly energy reports in the Voltline Home app
- Schedules and remote control from your phone
- Works with Amazon Alexa, Google Home and Matter

## Compatibility

- Works with 24 V systems: gas, oil and electric furnaces, central air, and heat pumps with auxiliary heat
- Supports up to 2 heating stages and 2 cooling stages
- Requires a C-wire
- Not compatible with line-voltage systems (120 V or 240 V), such as electric baseboard heaters

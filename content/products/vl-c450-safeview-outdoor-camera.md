# SafeView Outdoor Camera (VL-C450)

## Overview

The SafeView Outdoor Camera is wire-free and weatherproof. It runs on a rechargeable battery for up to 4 months of typical use, and its spotlight gives color video at night.

## Features

- 2K video (2560 by 1440 pixels)
- Color night vision with a built-in spotlight, up to 15 meters (49 feet)
- Rechargeable 6,000 mAh battery: up to 4 months of typical use per charge
- IP66 weather resistance, from minus 20 to 50 degrees Celsius (minus 4 to 122 degrees Fahrenheit)
- 100-decibel siren you can trigger from the app
- Person, vehicle and package detection
- Local recording to a microSD card up to 256 GB (card not included)

# Glide Wireless Mouse (VL-M250)

## Overview

Glide is a light, quiet wireless mouse. Its silent clicks suit shared offices, and one charge lasts up to 70 days.

- Six buttons, including back and forward
- Silent clicks
- Bluetooth or the included 2.4 GHz receiver
- Rechargeable: up to 70 days per charge over USB-C

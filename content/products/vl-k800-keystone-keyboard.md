# Keystone Mechanical Keyboard (VL-K800)

## Overview

Keystone is a compact 75% mechanical keyboard. It connects by Bluetooth to up to three devices, by its 2.4 GHz receiver, or by cable. The switches are hot-swappable, so you can change them without soldering.

## Features

- 75% layout with 84 keys
- Hot-swappable switch sockets for 3-pin and 5-pin switches
- Pre-lubricated Voltline Red linear switches
- Gasket-mounted for a softer, quieter typing feel
- Three ways to connect: Bluetooth 5.1 (up to 3 devices), 2.4 GHz receiver, USB-C cable
- 4,000 mAh battery: up to 200 hours with the backlight off, about 40 hours with it on
- PBT keycaps and per-key RGB backlight
- Keycaps for both Mac and Windows included

# Pulse Buds Pro (VL-A300)

## Overview

Pulse Buds Pro add active noise cancellation, a transparency mode and a wireless charging case to the Pulse Buds design. They connect to two devices at once, such as a phone and a laptop.

## Features

- Active noise cancellation that reduces noise by up to 35 decibels
- Transparency mode to hear your surroundings without removing the earbuds
- Up to 7 hours of playback with noise cancellation on, 10 hours with it off
- Up to 30 hours in total with the case, with noise cancellation on
- Wireless charging case (Qi) and USB-C charging
- Multipoint: connected to two devices at the same time
- IPX5 water resistance
- Six microphones for clear calls
- Bluetooth 5.4 with LDAC, AAC and SBC codecs

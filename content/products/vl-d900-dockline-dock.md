# Dockline 11-in-1 USB-C Dock (VL-D900)

## Overview

Dockline turns one USB-C port into a full desk setup: two displays, three USB-A ports, Ethernet, card readers and laptop charging.

## Features

- 11 ports from a single USB-C cable
- Up to two 4K displays at 60 Hz on Windows
- 100 W power passthrough, delivering up to 85 W to your laptop
- Gigabit Ethernet
- SD and microSD card readers

## Ports

- 2 x HDMI 2.0 (4K at 60 Hz)
- 3 x USB-A 3.2 (10 Gbps)
- 1 x USB-C 3.2 data port (10 Gbps)
- 1 x USB-C power input (100 W)
- 1 x Gigabit Ethernet
- 1 x SD card reader
- 1 x microSD card reader
- 1 x 3.5 mm audio

## Display support

- Windows laptops with DisplayPort Alt Mode: two external 4K displays at 60 Hz
- Mac with M1, M2, M3 or M4 base chips: one external display, because macOS limits these chips; the second HDMI port mirrors the first
- Mac with Pro or Max chips: two external displays

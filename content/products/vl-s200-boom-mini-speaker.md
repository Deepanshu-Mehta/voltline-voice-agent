# Boom Mini Speaker (VL-S200)

## Overview

Boom Mini is a small waterproof Bluetooth speaker for the beach, the pool or the shower. It is rated IP67, so it survives dust and being dropped in water, and it floats.

## Features

- 10 watts of output
- Up to 12 hours of playback per charge
- IP67 waterproof and dustproof; it floats
- PartyLink: pair two Boom Mini speakers for stereo sound
- Built-in microphone for speakerphone calls
- Bluetooth 5.3, USB-C charging

# ClearCam 4K Webcam (VL-W270)

## Overview

ClearCam is a plug-and-play 4K webcam for video calls and streaming. It needs no drivers and works with Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet.

## Features

- 4K at 30 frames per second, or 1080p at 60 frames per second
- Adjustable field of view: 65, 78 or 90 degrees
- Autofocus and automatic low-light correction
- Two noise-cancelling microphones
- Built-in privacy shutter
- USB-C, no drivers needed on Windows, macOS or ChromeOS

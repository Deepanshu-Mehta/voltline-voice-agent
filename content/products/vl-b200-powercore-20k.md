# PowerCore 20K Power Bank (VL-B200)

## Overview

PowerCore 20K charges a typical phone about four times. Its 30 W USB-C port can also charge tablets and small laptops. At 72 watt-hours it is under the 100 watt-hour airline limit, so you can take it in carry-on luggage.

## Features

- 20,000 mAh (72 Wh) capacity
- 30 W USB-C Power Delivery, input and output
- One extra USB-A port (18 W)
- Charges two devices at once
- Recharges fully in about 3.5 hours with a 30 W charger
- Allowed in airline carry-on bags; not allowed in checked bags

# ChargeMax 65W GaN Charger (VL-G650)

## Overview

ChargeMax is a compact wall charger with three ports. One port can charge a laptop at up to 65 watts, or you can charge three devices at once.

## Features

- 65 W from a single USB-C port
- Two USB-C ports and one USB-A port
- Gallium nitride (GaN) design, about half the size of a standard laptop charger
- Foldable prongs for travel
- Works worldwide from 100 to 240 volts (US plug)

## Power sharing

| Ports in use | Power |
| --- | --- |
| One USB-C | 65 W |
| Two USB-C | 45 W + 20 W |
| Two USB-C and USB-A | 45 W + 10 W + 10 W |

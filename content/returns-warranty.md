# Returns and warranty

## Return window

You can return most products within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. The product must be in like-new condition, with all accessories and the original packaging.

**Holiday returns:** orders delivered between 1 November and 31 December 2026 can be returned until 31 January 2027.

## Earbuds

For hygiene reasons, opened Pulse Buds (VL-A100) and Pulse Buds Pro (VL-A300) can be returned only if they are defective. Unopened earbuds follow the normal 30-day policy.

## Return shipping

- Defective or wrong item: return shipping is free.
- Change of mind: a $7.00 return label fee is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds

We refund to your original payment method within 5 business days of receiving your return. Your bank or card provider may take another 3 to 5 business days to show it.

## Exchanges

You can exchange a product for a different color of the same product within 30 days of delivery, free of charge.

## How to start a return

Open a support ticket with your order number, either through our voice agent or by email to support@voltline.example. We email you a return label within one business day.

## Limited warranty

Every Voltline product has a limited warranty against manufacturing defects:

| Product | Warranty |
| --- | --- |
| Horizon Headphones (VL-H500) | 24 months |
| ThermoSense Thermostat (VL-T700) | 24 months |
| All other products | 12 months |

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, including a battery that falls below 80% of its original capacity within the warranty period. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage beyond the product's rating, loss or theft. Voltline Care+ adds 2 years and one accidental damage claim; see Pricing and delivery.

## Making a warranty claim

Open a support ticket with your order number and a short description of the fault. We send a prepaid shipping label, and we repair or replace the product within 10 business days of receiving it.

# Orders and tracking

## Order numbers

Every order has a four-digit order number, shown in your confirmation email. Give this number to the voice agent to check your order.

## Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Processing | Payment received; the order has not shipped yet. It can still be changed or cancelled |
| Shipped | On its way; a tracking number has been emailed |
| Out for delivery | With the local courier and due today |
| Delivered | Delivered to your address |
| Cancelled | Cancelled before shipping; you have been refunded |
| Refunded | Returned and refunded |

## Carriers

We ship with UPS and FedEx. Your shipping email names the carrier and includes the tracking number.

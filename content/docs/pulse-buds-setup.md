# Pulse Buds and Pulse Buds Pro setup

## Pairing for the first time

1. Open the charging case with both earbuds inside.
2. Press and hold the button on the back of the case for 3 seconds, until the light flashes white.
3. On your phone, open Bluetooth settings and choose "Voltline Pulse Buds" or "Voltline Pulse Buds Pro".

## Connecting a second device (Pulse Buds Pro only)

Pulse Buds Pro can stay connected to two devices at once. With the first device connected, put the earbuds back in the case, hold the case button for 3 seconds, and pair the second device. Both devices then reconnect automatically.

## Touch controls

| Action | Pulse Buds | Pulse Buds Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play or pause | Tap once | Tap once |
| Next track | Tap twice | Tap twice |
| Voice assistant | Tap three times | Tap three times |
| Noise cancellation or transparency | Not available | Press and hold 2 seconds |

## Resetting the earbuds

If an earbud will not connect or plays in one ear only, reset them. Place both earbuds in the case, keep the lid open, and hold the case button for 15 seconds, until the light flashes amber three times. Then forget the earbuds in your Bluetooth settings and pair again.

## Firmware updates

Install the Voltline Sound app for iPhone or Android. It installs firmware updates and lets you adjust the equalizer.

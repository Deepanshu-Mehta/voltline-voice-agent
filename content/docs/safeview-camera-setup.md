# SafeView camera setup

## Before you start

- Install the Voltline Home app for iPhone or Android and create an account.
- SafeView cameras connect to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only. If your router combines both bands under one name, the camera picks the 2.4 GHz band automatically. If setup fails, temporarily turn off the 5 GHz band.
- Charge the SafeView Outdoor Camera fully before mounting it, which takes about 6 hours.

## Adding the camera

1. Power on the camera. The light blinks blue when it is ready to set up.
2. In the Voltline Home app, tap Add device and scan the QR code on the camera.
3. Choose your Wi-Fi network and enter the password.
4. Hold your phone's screen 15 to 20 centimeters in front of the camera so it can read the setup code. You hear "Connected" when it is done.

## Recording options

- **microSD card:** insert a card of up to 256 GB to record motion events locally. This is free.
- **Voltline Secure plan:** $3.99 a month or $39.99 a year. It keeps 30 days of cloud video history for up to 4 cameras.

## Status lights

| Light | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Blinking blue | Ready to set up |
| Solid blue | Connected and working |
| Blinking red | Wi-Fi connection lost |
| Solid red | Starting up or updating; do not unplug |

## Resetting a camera

Press and hold the reset button for 10 seconds, until you hear a chime. The camera returns to setup mode.

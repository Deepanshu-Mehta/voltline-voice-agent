# Dockline display setup

## Connecting the dock

1. Plug your laptop's USB-C charger into the dock's power input port (marked with a lightning bolt).
2. Connect the dock's attached cable to a USB-C port on your laptop that supports video (DisplayPort Alt Mode or Thunderbolt).
3. Connect your displays to the two HDMI ports.

## How many displays you can use

| Laptop | External displays |
| --- | --- |
| Windows laptop with DisplayPort Alt Mode | Two, up to 4K at 60 Hz each |
| Mac with an M1, M2, M3 or M4 base chip | One; the second HDMI port mirrors the first |
| Mac with a Pro or Max chip | Two |

## Charging through the dock

The dock accepts up to 100 W from your charger and passes up to 85 W to your laptop. The rest powers the dock and its ports. A 100 W USB-C charger gives the best results.

## Troubleshooting

- No picture: check that your laptop's USB-C port supports video; some ports are data only.
- Display at 30 Hz: use an HDMI 2.0 cable rated for 4K at 60 Hz.
- Ethernet not working on a Mac: no driver is needed; restart the Mac after first connecting the dock.

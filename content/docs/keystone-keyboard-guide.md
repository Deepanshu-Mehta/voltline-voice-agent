# Keystone keyboard guide

## Connecting

- **Bluetooth:** press Fn + 1, Fn + 2 or Fn + 3 for 3 seconds to pair to that slot. Tap the same keys to switch between devices.
- **2.4 GHz receiver:** plug the receiver into your computer and press Fn + 4.
- **USB-C cable:** plug it in; the keyboard switches to wired mode and charges.

## Mac or Windows

Press Fn + M for Mac layout or Fn + W for Windows layout. Swap in the included Mac keycaps if you like.

## Backlight and battery

- Fn + Space cycles backlight effects, and Fn + Up or Down changes brightness.
- The battery lasts up to 200 hours with the backlight off and about 40 hours with it on.
- The Esc key flashes red when the battery is below 10%.

## Changing switches

Keystone has hot-swappable sockets for 3-pin and 5-pin mechanical switches. Use the included puller to remove a keycap, then pull the switch straight up. Push the new switch straight down until it clicks. No soldering is needed.

## Factory reset

Hold Fn + Esc for 5 seconds until the backlight flashes white three times.

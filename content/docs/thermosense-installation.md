# ThermoSense installation

## Check compatibility first

ThermoSense works with 24-volt heating and cooling systems, including gas, oil and electric furnaces, central air, and heat pumps with auxiliary heat. It supports up to 2 heating stages and 2 cooling stages.

It does not work with line-voltage systems (120 V or 240 V), such as electric baseboard heaters. If your current thermostat has thick wires connected with wire nuts, it is probably line voltage.

ThermoSense needs a C-wire for power. Remove your old thermostat's cover and look for a wire on the terminal marked C. A C-wire adapter is not included.

## Installation steps

1. Turn off power to your heating and cooling system at the breaker.
2. Take a photo of your old thermostat's wiring, then label each wire with the included labels.
3. Remove the old thermostat and fit the ThermoSense wall plate.
4. Connect each labeled wire to the matching terminal.
5. Attach the thermostat, turn the breaker back on, and follow the setup on the screen.
6. Add the thermostat in the Voltline Home app to control it from your phone.

Installation takes about 30 minutes. If you are not comfortable with wiring, use a qualified HVAC technician.

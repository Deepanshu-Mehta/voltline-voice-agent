# About Voltline Electronics

## Who we are

Voltline Electronics designs everyday audio, smart home, computing and charging products and sells them directly online. We were founded in 2019 in Austin, Texas, and we have 42 employees. Voltline is a fictional company created for a voice agent demonstration.

## What we believe

- Products should be simple to set up and last for years.
- Specifications should be stated plainly, without inflated numbers.
- Support should be easy to reach, by voice, phone or email.

## Contact and support hours

| Channel | Availability |
| --- | --- |

| Human support team | Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. Eastern Time; Saturday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Eastern Time; closed Sunday |

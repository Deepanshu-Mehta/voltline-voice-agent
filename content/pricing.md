# Pricing and delivery

## Prices

All prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax, which is calculated at checkout from your delivery address. Current prices for every product are listed on the Products page.

## Delivery options

We ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship outside the United States.

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3 to 5 business days | Free on orders of $50 or more; $5.99 below $50 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $14.99 |
| Next day | Next business day | $24.99; order by 2 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday to Friday |

- Orders to Alaska and Hawaii ship by Standard delivery only and take 5 to 8 business days.
- Express and Next day are not available for PO boxes.
- Orders placed before 2 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Later orders ship the next business day.
- Every shipment gets a tracking number by email, and you can ask our voice agent for your order status at any time.

## Discounts and offers

- **Student discount:** 10% off one order per month for verified students. Verify with your school email at checkout. It cannot be combined with other offers.
- **Home Starter Bundle:** the SafeView Indoor Camera and the Smart Plug 2-Pack together for $114.99, a saving of $9.99.
- **Price match:** if a major US retailer lowers its price on the identical new product within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference. Ask our support team with your order number.

We do not run other discount codes. Codes found on coupon websites are not issued by Voltline.

## Voltline Care+

Voltline Care+ extends your warranty by 2 years and adds cover for one accidental damage claim, such as a drop or a spill.

| Product price | Care+ price |
| --- | --- |
| Under $100 | $19 |
| $100 or more | $29 |

You can add Care+ at checkout or within 30 days of delivery.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Orders over $150 can be paid in 4 interest-free installments with Affirm.

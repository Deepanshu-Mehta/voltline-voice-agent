# Frequently asked questions

## Orders and delivery

### How do I track my order?

Your order number is a four-digit number in your confirmation email. Ask our voice agent for the status of that order number, or use the tracking link in your shipping email.

### Can I change or cancel my order?

You can cancel or change an order while its status is Processing. Once it has shipped, you can return it instead. Ask the voice agent to open a support ticket for a change or cancellation.

### Do you ship internationally?

No. We ship only within the United States, including Alaska and Hawaii.

### How much is shipping?

Standard delivery is free on orders of $50 or more and $5.99 below that. Express costs $14.99 and Next day costs $24.99.

### When will my order ship?

Orders placed before 2 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Later orders ship the next business day.

### Can I pick up an order in person?

No. Voltline sells online only and has no retail stores.

## Returns and refunds

### What is your return policy?

You can return most products within 30 days of delivery, in like-new condition with all accessories. Orders delivered between 1 November and 31 December 2026 can be returned until 31 January 2027.

### Can I return earbuds I have opened?

Opened Pulse Buds and Pulse Buds Pro can be returned only if they are defective, for hygiene reasons.

### How long does a refund take?

We refund within 5 business days of receiving your return. Your bank may take another 3 to 5 business days.

### Is return shipping free?

Return shipping is free for defective or wrong items. For a change of mind, a $7.00 label fee is deducted from your refund.

## Warranty and Care+

### How long is the warranty?

Horizon Headphones and the ThermoSense Thermostat have a 24-month warranty. All other products have 12 months.

### Does the warranty cover accidental damage?

No. The standard warranty covers manufacturing defects only. Voltline Care+ adds one accidental damage claim and 2 extra years.

### How much is Voltline Care+?

$19 for products under $100 and $29 for products of $100 or more. You can add it at checkout or within 30 days of delivery.

## Products

### Do Pulse Buds have noise cancellation?

The standard Pulse Buds (VL-A100) do not. Pulse Buds Pro (VL-A300) have active noise cancellation of up to 35 decibels.

### When will Horizon Headphones be back in stock?

The next shipment of Horizon Headphones is expected on 4 December 2026.

### Do SafeView cameras work with 5 GHz Wi-Fi?

No. SafeView cameras connect to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only. Most routers broadcast both bands, so you can usually connect to the 2.4 GHz band.

### Do I need a subscription for SafeView cameras?

No. Live view, alerts and microSD recording are free. A Voltline Secure plan adds 30 days of cloud video history.

### Will ThermoSense work with my baseboard heaters?

No. ThermoSense works with 24-volt systems and does not support line-voltage baseboard heaters. It also needs a C-wire.

### Can the Dockline dock run two monitors from my MacBook?

On a Mac with a base M1, M2, M3 or M4 chip, macOS allows only one external display. Macs with Pro or Max chips, and most Windows laptops, can run two 4K displays at 60 Hz.

### Can I take the PowerCore 20K on a plane?

Yes, in carry-on luggage. At 72 watt-hours it is under the 100 watt-hour airline limit. Power banks are not allowed in checked bags.

### Does the Keystone keyboard work with a Mac?

Yes. Keystone works with Mac and Windows, and Mac keycaps are included.

### Does the ChargeMax charger come with a cable?

No. ChargeMax ships without a cable.

### Do your smart plugs work with Apple Home?

Yes. The Smart Plug 2-Pack supports Matter, so it works with Apple Home, Amazon Alexa and Google Home.

## Payments and discounts

### Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Orders over $150 can be paid in 4 installments with Affirm.

### Do you have a student discount?

Yes. Verified students get 10% off one order per month. It cannot be combined with other offers.

### Do you price match?

Yes. If a major US retailer lowers its price on the identical new product within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference.

### Do you have discount codes?

No. Apart from the student discount, the Home Starter Bundle and price matching, we do not issue discount codes.

## Support

### When is your support team available?

Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. Eastern Time, and Saturday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Eastern Time. The voice agent is available at all times.

### Can someone call me back?

Yes. Give the voice agent your name, phone number and a preferred time, and our support team will call you back during opening hours.
